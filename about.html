Our Story

Built on Quality,
Driven by Purpose

Green Optical Lab Ltd. was founded with a clear mission: to make genuine, high-quality optical lenses accessible to every optical shop and optician across Bangladesh — without the prohibitive cost of traditional import chains.

We established direct relationships with certified lens manufacturers in China, allowing us to import authentic products at source pricing and pass those savings on to our customers throughout Bangladesh.

Recognising that importing alone wasn't enough, we invested in building our own in-house production lab — a facility capable of custom-producing large volumes of lenses to exact prescriptions and specifications.

Our advanced storage and inventory system ensures that every lens is catalogued, maintained, and ready for fast dispatch — and through partnerships with every major courier service in Bangladesh, we deliver to all 64 districts without exception.

  • Certified imports directly from Chinese manufacturers
  • Own production lab for custom large-volume orders
  • Systematic storage for fast, accurate fulfilment
  • All 64 districts covered via multiple courier partners
  • Wholesale bulk pricing for retailers and opticians
